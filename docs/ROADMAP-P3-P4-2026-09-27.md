# Denty Roadmap P3 + P4 — 2026-09-27

## P3: regulated/external capability foundations

Implemented as safe domain and persistence foundations, not as a claim of legal certification or provider activation.

- Fiscal/SIF foundation: immutable-style chained fiscal record primitive plus `fiscal_records` persistence. The final AEAT XML, QR, declaration, submission and error catalogue remain provider/regulatory adapter work.
- Reconciliation: deterministic payment↔bank matching primitive and `bank_transactions` ingestion table.
- Private e-prescription: provider-neutral dispatch contract. It refuses unsigned prescriptions. Actual provider certification/integration remains external.
- Radiographic AI: results can only enter `requires_human_review`; they cannot automatically write a diagnosis or treatment plan.
- Reception kiosk: token-gated check-in maps to `arrived` and is auditable.
- Integration event inbox/outbox foundation with idempotency and retry metadata.

## P4: interoperability, resilience and automation

P4 is defined here as the platform-hardening layer that makes later integrations safe to operate at scale.

- Offline/local-first conflict policy: higher version wins; timestamp only breaks equal-version conflicts. Clinical signed records must remain immutable/versioned rather than merged silently.
- Idempotency keys for provider/webhook processing.
- FHIR R5 export primitives for `DocumentReference` and `AuditEvent`. These are interoperability mappings, not a claim of conformance to a Spanish implementation guide.
- Recall campaign job builder with deterministic idempotency keys.
- Persistence for interoperability export audit.
- Supabase-ready queue/event table for retries and dead-letter handling at the application layer.

## Current-source architecture notes

- AEAT distinguishes VERI*FACTU and non-VERI*FACTU SIF. Both require traceability/record chaining; non-VERI*FACTU additionally requires stronger local controls such as event records. Denty therefore keeps fiscal logic behind an adapter boundary and does not label the current foundation as certified.
- Supabase Cron/pg_cron plus Edge Functions can schedule provider jobs. Queues can be consumed by Edge Functions and retried by leaving failed messages undeleted. Secrets belong in Vault/server-side configuration.
- FHIR R5 `DocumentReference` is suitable for document metadata/context, while `AuditEvent` models who/what/when/why for auditable events.

## Explicit non-goals for this increment

- No production AEAT submission without the exact current schemas, signing/QR requirements, test environment and legal acceptance criteria.
- No autonomous radiographic diagnosis.
- No prescription transmission without an approved provider contract and required signing workflow.
- No silent conflict merge of signed clinical documents.
